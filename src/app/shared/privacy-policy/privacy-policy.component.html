Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Welcome to The Taqwa Tracker ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and providing a secure environment for your spiritual journey. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our Progressive Web App (PWA).

2. Information Collection

Account Information

When you register, we collect your email address and name to create and manage your account, track your reading streaks, and personalize your experience.

Location Data

We request access to your device's geolocation to provide accurate prayer times and Qibla direction based on your current coordinate. This data is processed locally or sent to our API for location resolution (OpenStreetMap) and is not stored permanently unless you explicitly save a favorite location.

3. Use of Information

  • To provide and maintain the core features of the app (Prayer times, Quran, Hadith).
  • To personalize your experience and track your progress (e.g., Quran reading streaks).
  • To communicate with you regarding your account or provide updates.
  • To monitor and analyze usage patterns to improve our services.

4. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party services to enhance our platform:

  • Backend & Auth: Managed via our secure API at api.thetaqwatracker.com.
  • Location Services: OpenStreetMap Nominatim for address resolution.
  • AI Services: Backend agentic workflows for the Islamic Chatbot.
  • Content Delivery: GitHub Raw for serving library PDF documents.

5. Data Security

We implement a variety of security measures to maintain the safety of your personal information. Your data is protected by industry-standard encryption and secure authentication protocols. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

6. Cookies and Local Storage

We use browser local storage and cookies to maintain your session and store preferences (like theme selection or last read ayah) locally on your device for a fast and seamless experience.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the Feedback section in the app menu.
